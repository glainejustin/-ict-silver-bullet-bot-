# Property Sourcing Fee Agreement — Starter Template

> ⚠️ **This is a plain-English starting draft, not a legal document.** Have
> a solicitor review and adapt it before using it with a real client — it
> doesn't account for your specific structure or recent case law, and using
> an unreviewed contract with real money involved is a real risk. Many UK
> property investor associations also publish member templates that are
> worth comparing this against.

---

## PROPERTY SOURCING AGREEMENT

**Between:**
`[Your Business Name]` ("the Sourcer")
and
`[Investor/Buyer Name]` ("the Client")

**Date:** `[Date]`

### 1. Services
The Sourcer agrees to identify and present property investment opportunities
("Deals") to the Client, matching the Client's stated criteria (area, budget,
strategy, minimum return), for the Client's independent consideration.

### 2. Client acknowledgement
The Client acknowledges that:
- The Sourcer is **not** providing regulated financial or investment advice.
- All figures provided (valuation, BMV%, refurbishment estimates, projected
  ROI/yield, rental income) are **estimates only**, based on third-party
  data (e.g. HM Land Registry sold prices) and the Sourcer's experience, and
  **must be independently verified** by the Client (e.g. via an RICS
  valuation, a qualified surveyor, and their own solicitor) before proceeding.
- The Client is responsible for conducting their own due diligence before
  exchanging contracts on any Deal.

### 3. Sourcing fee
- The Client agrees to pay the Sourcer a fee of `[£X / X% of purchase price]`
  upon **legal completion** of a purchase of any Deal introduced by the
  Sourcer, payable within `[X]` days of completion.
- No fee is payable if the Client does not proceed to completion.
- `[Optional: non-refundable deposit on reservation of a specific Deal, if used — clearly state the amount and conditions.]`

### 4. Exclusivity window
Once the Sourcer introduces a specific Deal to the Client, the Client agrees
not to pursue that specific property directly or via another party for a
period of `[X]` months without the Sourcer's fee becoming due, to prevent
circumvention of the agreed fee.

### 5. Data protection
The Sourcer will handle the Client's personal data in accordance with UK
GDPR and will not share it with third parties except as needed to complete
a transaction, consistent with the Sourcer's privacy policy.

### 6. Term & termination
Either party may terminate this agreement in writing with `[X]` days' notice.
Termination does not affect fees already due on Deals introduced before
termination.

### 7. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.

**Signed:**

Sourcer: ___________________________ Date: ___________

## Notes for you (the Sourcer) — delete before sending
- Decide and clearly state your fee structure **before** you ever send a
  deal: flat fee, % of purchase price, or % of GDV — pick one, be consistent.
- Never take a client's money before they've independently verified the
  numbers — this protects you as much as them.
- Keep a dated, timestamped record of exactly when you introduced each Deal
  to each Client (this project's `outreach` table already does this for
  you) — it's your evidence if a fee dispute ever arises.
- If you also handle any client money (rare for sourcing, more common for
  management), get specific advice on client money protection rules first.
