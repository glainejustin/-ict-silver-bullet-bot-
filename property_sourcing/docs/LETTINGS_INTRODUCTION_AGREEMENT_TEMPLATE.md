# Landlord Tenant-Introduction Agreement — Starter Template

> ⚠️ **Not a legal document — get it reviewed by a solicitor before real
> use.** This is a starting draft for a simple, low-risk model: you
> introduce a tenant, the landlord deals directly with that tenant for rent,
> deposit and the tenancy agreement itself, and pays you a one-off
> commission on signing. This deliberately avoids you ever holding client
> money (see `LETTINGS_LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md`).

---

## TENANT INTRODUCTION AGREEMENT

**Between:** `[Your Business Name]` ("the Introducer")
**And:** `[Landlord Name]` ("the Landlord")
**Date:** `[Date]`
**Property:** `[Property Address]`

### 1. Instruction
The Landlord instructs the Introducer, on a `[sole / multi-agency]` basis,
to introduce prospective tenants for the Property.

### 2. What the Introducer does and does not do
- The Introducer will identify and introduce prospective tenants matching
  the Landlord's stated criteria.
- The Introducer does **not** draft or manage the tenancy agreement, collect
  rent, hold or protect any deposit, or carry out Right to Rent checks —
  these remain the Landlord's responsibility (or their appointed managing
  agent's) at all times.
- The Landlord confirms they will carry out all legally required checks
  (Right to Rent, references, deposit protection if applicable) before or at
  the start of any tenancy arising from an introduction.

### 3. Commission
The Landlord agrees to pay the Introducer a commission of
`[one month's rent / X% of the first year's rent / flat fee of £X]` upon the
Landlord signing a tenancy agreement with a tenant introduced by the
Introducer, payable within `[X]` days of the tenancy start date.

### 4. No fees to the tenant
The Introducer will not charge the introduced tenant any fee prohibited
under the Tenant Fees Act 2019.

### 5. Introduction period
If the Landlord lets the Property to a tenant introduced by the Introducer
within `[X]` months of that introduction — even if arranged directly without
the Introducer's further involvement — the commission under clause 3 remains
payable.

### 6. Data protection
Both parties will handle any personal data shared under this agreement in
accordance with UK GDPR.

### 7. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
